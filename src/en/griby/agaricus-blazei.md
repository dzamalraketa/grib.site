## Identification

An Agaricus species with a distinctive almond scent; cultivated widely in Japan and Brazil.

## Research status

Beta-glucan extracts studied in randomized trials (inflammatory bowel disease symptoms, immune markers); results are preliminary but human-grade.

## In practice

- The almond smell distinguishes it from other Agaricus.
- Culinary use is safe; supplement claims outpace evidence.
