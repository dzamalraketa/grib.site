## Identification

Big white funnel russula: dense flesh, white latex-like juice, gills spotting brown. Brittle.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — for salting with soaking, like milkcaps. Not for frying fresh.

## In practice

- White + dense + spotting gills + acrid milk = belyanka.
- Compare [skripitsa](/en/griby/skripitsa/) — same tribe, decurrent gills.
