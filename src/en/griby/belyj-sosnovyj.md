## Identification

Like a porcini but under pine and usually earlier: cap darker, wine-red-brown; stem with a white net; flesh white, unchanging.

## Edibility

Fully edible, same culinary uses as [porcini](/en/griby/borovik/).

## In practice

- In pine forests it often comes weeks before the main porcini wave.
- Same lookalike checks: pink tubes → bitter bolete; red pores → discard.
