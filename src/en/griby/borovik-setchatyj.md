## Identification

Close to the porcini: lighter, often cracked cap; the white net on the stem reaches higher; flesh white, unchanging. Mycorrhizal with broadleaf trees.

## Edibility

Fully edible; culinary value equal to porcini.

## In practice

- Among the first quality boletes of the year in oak woods.
- Distinct from [pale boletes with red pores](/en/griby/satanskij-grib/) by the white net and no blue staining.
