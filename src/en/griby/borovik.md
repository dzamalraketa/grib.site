## Identification

Brown convex cap, thick stem with a white raised net, tubes white → olive with age. Flesh white, does not darken on the cut; smell nutty and pleasant.

## Edibility

A benchmark edible: fresh, dried, pickled, frozen. The broth and dried porcini aroma are signature. High in protein for a mushroom and widely studied nutritionally.

## In practice

- Check the tube surface: pinkish → it's the [bitter bolete](/en/griby/zhelchnyj-grib/); red pores + blue staining → [Satan's bolete](/en/griby/satanskij-grib/).
- Wormy stems are trimmed; for drying, caps are sliced without washing.
