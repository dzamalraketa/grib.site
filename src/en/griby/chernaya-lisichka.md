## Identification

Dark brown-to-black hollow trumpet, scaly cap surface, underside smooth to wrinkled. No gills, no ring. Camouflages against leaf litter.

## Edibility

A gourmet edible; especially prized dried and ground — the aroma intensifies, used like a seasoning.

## In practice

- Hard to see but very safe: nothing dangerous looks like a black hollow trumpet.
- Cut, don't pull — mycelium matters.
