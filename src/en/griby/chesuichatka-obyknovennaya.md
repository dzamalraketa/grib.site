## Identification

Clusters of yellowish caps densely covered with upturned dark scales; gills yellowish → rusty. Flesh smells radishy and tastes bitter. Grows in large tufts on stumps and roots.

## Why it is dangerous

Gastrointestinal poisoning: vomiting, diarrhea, cramps. Beyond that, the flesh is bitter and woody — it spoils food even in small doses.

## How not to confuse it

| Feature | [Golden scalycap](/en/griby/chesuichatka-zolotistaya/) | Common scalycap |
|---|---|---|
| Cap color | golden-rusty, dry | yellowish, sticky |
| Scales | recurved, sparser | dense, shaggy, upturned |
| Taste | mild | bitter |
| Spore print | rusty | rusty — useless for separating |
