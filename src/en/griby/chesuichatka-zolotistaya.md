## Identification

Golden, sticky, scale-covered caps in clusters on wood; rusty-brown spores.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible after full cooking; gastrointestinal reactions are documented in some people. Many guides treat it as "better avoided."

## In practice

- Golden + slimy + scaly + wood cluster = golden scalycap.
- If in doubt — leave it; the poisonous lookalike space is dense.
