## Identification

Club-shaped fruiting bodies emerging from buried cicada nymphs — a striking parasitoid.

## Research

Polysaccharides studied for immunomodulatory and kidney-protective effects — mostly preclinical; a few small human studies exist.

## In practice

- Used in traditional Chinese medicine; quality products come from verified cultivation.
