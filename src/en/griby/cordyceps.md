## Identification

Bright orange club-shaped bodies; wild on insect pupae, cultivated on grain.

## Key compound

Cordycepin (3'-deoxyadenosine) — studied for metabolic and exercise effects; human trials on VO2max show mixed results.

## In practice

- Cultivated militaris ≠ wild sinensis — different products, different prices.
- Culinary/supplement use is considered safe at studied doses.
