## Identification

Pear-shaped, 2–4 cm, whitish → brown; grows in clusters on dead wood — a distinctive substrate. Inside: white gleba when young.

## Edibility

Edible young and white; good sauteed. Once the inside yellows — inedible.

## In practice

- On wood + pear shape + white inside = this species.
- Slice every one before the pan.
