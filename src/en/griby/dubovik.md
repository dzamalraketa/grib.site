## Identification

Olive-brown cap, orange-red pores, thick netted stem; flesh yellowish, **turning blue within seconds** when cut.

## Edibility — conditional

Edible only after thorough cooking; raw or undercooked it causes gastrointestinal poisoning. Some sources also warn about alcohol taken with it. In Russian tradition it is eaten; western guides are more cautious.

## In practice

- Red-pored boletes are for those who know the species exactly.
- Never raw, no "salad from lurid bolete" — cooking is mandatory.
