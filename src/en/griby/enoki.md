## Identification

Winter wood-cluster mushroom: small brown-orange caps, dark velvety stems, white gills. The cultivated white needle-shape is grown in darkness — a different look, same species.

## Edibility

Edible cooked; the cultivated form is a staple. Wild velvet shank is a late-season prize.

## Safety note

Cultivated enoki has caused documented listeriosis outbreaks (US, 2020s) — the risk is in ready-to-eat handling, not the species; cook before eating.

## In practice

- Brown velvet stem + winter + wood = velvet shank.
