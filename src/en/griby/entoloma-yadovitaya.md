## Identification

A large whitish-gray mushroom: gills cream at first, then salmon-pink; flesh with a mealy smell. Salmon gills on a mature specimen are the *Entoloma* signature.

## Why it is dangerous

Gastrointestinal poisoning 30 minutes to 2 hours after eating: vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain — severe and lasting days. A Turkish case series documented hospitalizations. Western guides (First Nature) call it "deadly poisonous"; Wikipedia notes deaths are not typical — we class it as dangerous by precaution.

## How not to confuse it

- With [St George's mushroom](/en/griby/kalokibe-majskij/): check gill color — pink means discard.
- With the [field mushroom](/en/griby/shampinon-polevoj/): champignons have brown gills and a proper ring.
