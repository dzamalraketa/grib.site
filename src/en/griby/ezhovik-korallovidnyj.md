## Description

Fruit body 10–25 cm, white, coral-branched: limbs spread from a common base, hung with cascading spines up to 1 cm. Grows on dead hardwood. Yellows and browns with age. Flesh white, firm, without a marked smell.

## Kinship with lion's mane

The closest relative of *H. erinaceus* — the mushroom behind "brain-boosting" supplements. *H. coralloides* also contains hericenones and erinacines — compounds that stimulate NGF synthesis in experiments. Unlike cultivated *erinaceus*, the coral tooth is gathered only in the wild — and rarely.

## Conservation status

Red-listed in several European countries and Russian regions: it suffers from the felling of old-growth broadleaf woodland where it persists. Collection is prohibited in protected areas.

## Cooking

Edible and prized: sweet-nutty flavour, seafood-like texture — minced coral tooth fries up like "crab cakes". The safety rule here is ecological: **the species is red-listed** — if it is protected in your region, photograph it and leave it be.
