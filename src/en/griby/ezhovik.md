## Identification

White fruit body of cascading spines on wood — unmistakable.

## What research says

Studied for erinacines/hericenones and nerve growth factor stimulation: mostly in vitro and animal data; small human trials exist on cognition and mood, with mixed results. Interest is real, proof is not.

## Edibility

Edible and meaty — a seafood-like texture; sautéed slices are a common preparation.

## In practice

- Culinary: edible. Supplemental: evidence still developing.
