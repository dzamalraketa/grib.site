## Description

Fruit body — a bracket 10–60 cm and more, semicircular, hard-woody; upper surface grey-brown with concentric ridges, matt (no reishi lacquer!). **The underside is white** — and at the slightest touch or scratch **browns instantly**: that is what the "drawing" on the mushroom is built on.

## A white canvas you cannot erase

The pores are white, but the flesh beneath contains enzymes oxidising on air: a scratch browns in seconds and forever. On the underside of a large conk you can "write" — hence "artist's conk". The drawings persist for years while the bracket lives — and it can live for decades.

## Ecology — the base predator

A parasite and saprotroph: it colonises weakened broadleaf trees near the trunk base (beech, oak, maple), causing **white rot** — breaking down lignin. After the host dies it keeps fruiting on the dead wood for years. A single conk can live **50+ years**, adding a layer of pores each year.

## Reishi's relative

The same genus as true reishi (*Ganoderma lucidum*) — but without the shiny lacquer and without medicinal value. "Inedible" is a technicality: you cannot eat wood — though folk medicine brews teas from it too.
