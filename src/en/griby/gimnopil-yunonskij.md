## Identification

Large clustered mushroom on wood: cap orange-rusty, scaly; gills rusty; flesh bitter. Rusty spore print. Spectacular size and color — and it is poisonous.

## Why it is dangerous

Contains gymnopilin and neurotropic (in some collections hallucinogenic) compounds; Japanese literature records "laughing mushroom" poisonings — delirium, nausea, gastrointestinal syndrome. Documented severe courses.

## In practice

- Big rusty clusters on stumps are not honey fungi: check the rusty gills and bitter flesh.
- Confused with [golden scalycap](/en/griby/chesuichatka-zolotistaya/) and honey fungi — when in doubt, skip.
