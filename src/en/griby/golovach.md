## Identification

A white ball the size of a football or bigger, on soil; interior uniform and white.

## Edibility

Edible while completely white inside; sliced and fried like steaks. Once yellowing begins inside — inedible.

## In practice

- Always cut it: marbled or yellowed interior → discard.
- White + huge + uniform interior = safe target.
