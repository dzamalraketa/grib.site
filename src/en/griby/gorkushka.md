## Identification

Red-brown cap with a central umbo; white latex turning faintly yellow; extremely acrid taste.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible: salting after prolonged soaking. The acrid latex is a ciliary irritant — never fresh.

## In practice

- Acrid latex + umbo + conifer forest = rufous milkcap.
