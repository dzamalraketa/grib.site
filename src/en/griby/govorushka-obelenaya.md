## Identification

Small white-gray funnel caps on soil in rings; gills whitish, decurrent; stem short. Confused with edible field species — including the [St George's mushroom](/en/griby/kalokibe-majskij/) and young [meadow mushroom](/en/griby/opyonok-lugovoj/).

## Why it is dangerous

A rich source of **muscarine**: within 15 min – 2 h — sweating, salivation, tearing, abdominal cramps, vomiting, diarrhea; pulse slows, blood pressure falls. Severe courses and deaths are documented in the literature.

## In practice

- Small white funnels growing in rings on lawns — skip entirely.
- Muscarinic symptoms after mushrooms → emergency services immediately; atropine is the specific antidote.
