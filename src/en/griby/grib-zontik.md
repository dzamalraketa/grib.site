## Identification

Large umbrella-cap with brown scales on white, snakeskin-patterned stem, a **movable ring**, white flesh that doesn't change color.

## Edibility

A choice edible — cap only; the stem is woody. Fried like a schnitzel is classic.

## In practice — the key rule

- Take only large, opened parasols (cap 10 cm+): the deadly [fatal dapperling](/en/griby/lepiota-korichnevo-krasnaya/) is a small lookalike with an amatoxin load.
- Small scaly "parasols" = never.
