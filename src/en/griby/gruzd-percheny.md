## Identification

White funnel, very crowded decurrent gills, abundant white acrid latex.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — salted after soaking; dried and ground as a pepper substitute historically.

## In practice

- The latex's fierce pepperiness + crowded decurrent gills = peppery milkcap.
