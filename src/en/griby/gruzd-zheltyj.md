## Identification

Yellowish cap, scrobiculate (pitted) stem, latex white → yellow on air.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — salting after soaking.

## In practice

- Yellow-staining latex + pitted stem = yellow milkcap, not the [true milkcap](/en/griby/gruzd/).
