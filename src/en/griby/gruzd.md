## Identification

White-cream funnel with a fringed margin; latex white, not changing color; acrid.

## Edibility — conditional

The salting classic — soaked 1–2 days, salted. Raw it's unpalatably acrid.

## In practice

- Fringe + unchanging white latex + birch woods = true milkcap.
- [Yellow milkcap](/en/griby/gruzd-zheltyj/): latex stains yellow, spruce woods.
