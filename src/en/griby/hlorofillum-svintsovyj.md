## Description

A large white parasol: cap 7–30 cm with brown scales on a white ground, gills white then **grey-green when mature**, a ring on the stem. The key mark is the **green spore print** — no other large gilled mushroom has one.

## America's #1 poisoning mushroom

In the US and Canada *C. molybdites* causes more recorded poisonings than any other mushroom: people pick it as a "parasol" straight off the lawn. Symptoms — violent gastroenteritis within 1–3 hours: vomiting, diarrhea (occasionally bloody), dehydration, cramps; rarely fatal but may need IV fluids.

## Lookalikes

| Mushroom | Spore print | Mature gills |
|---|---|---|
| **C. molybdites** | **green** | grey-green |
| Parasol (*M. procera*) | white | white |
| Shaggy parasol (*C. rhacodes*) | white | white, flesh blushes |
| Agaricus | brown-purple | pink → brown |

## Practice

Take a spore print from every "parasol": white powder = parasols and Agaricus, **green = discard at once**. Young fruit bodies show barely a hint of green before the gills mature — so the rule is "check mature gills" plus a spore print.
