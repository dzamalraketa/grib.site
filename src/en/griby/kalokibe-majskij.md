## Identification

Cream-white thick cap, dense white gills, short stem; strong mealy (fresh-flour) smell. Appears in April–June — the season of the first edible spring cap mushrooms.

## Edibility

Good edible — fried, stewed. The early season makes it popular.

## How not to confuse it

- [Livid entoloma](/en/griby/entoloma-yadovitaya/): salmon-pink gills when mature, no mealy smell — discard.
- [Fool's funnel](/en/griby/govorushka-obelenaya/): thin flesh, decurrent gills, poisonous.
