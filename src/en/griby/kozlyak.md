## Identification

Small soft mushroom: reddish-brown sticky cap, large angular pores that stain, no ring. Under pines, often in moss.

## Edibility

Edible, mild; best young — old fruit bodies turn slimy and bland.

## In practice

- Grows shoulder-to-shoulder with slippery jacks in pine woods.
