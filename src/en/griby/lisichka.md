## Identification

Egg-yellow funnel; the underside has **blunt, forked ridges** that run down the stem — not thin gills. Flesh dense, white inside, smells faintly of apricot. Famously worm-resistant.

## Edibility

A top edible: fried, stewed, pickled. Rich in vitamin D precursors among mushrooms.

## How not to confuse it

| Feature | Chanterelle | [False chanterelle](/en/griby/lozhnaya-lisichka/) |
|---|---|---|
| Underside | blunt ridges | thin true gills |
| Substrate | soil | rotting wood |
| Flesh | dense, fruity | soft, no aroma |
