## Identification

Large clustered fronds, gray-brown, at oak bases — looks like a nested hen.

## Edibility

Choice edible — fronds sautéed or roasted; firm texture holds up.

## Research

Beta-glucan fractions studied in human trials on immune markers; evidence is preliminary — an interesting culinary-medicinal species, not a therapy.
