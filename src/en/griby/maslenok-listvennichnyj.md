## Identification

Bright chrome-yellow to orange slimy cap 4–10 cm; yellow angular pores; stipe with a gelatinous ring (white → purplish-brown with age) and glandular dots above it. **Only under larch.**

## Edibility

Edible after removing the slimy cap skin (it can upset the stomach); cook through. Frequently maggot-infested even when young — check stems.

## In practice

- Look under every larch within its crown radius — parks and avenues fruit as heavily as forests.
- Confusion with other *Suillus* is harmless — all are edible — but the gold cap + purple-brown ring + larch trio marks this one.
