## Identification

Brown slimy cap (peels off when cooked), yellowish pores, ring on the stem in S. luteus. Grows under pines in troops from early summer.

## Edibility

Edible after removing the slimy cap skin — the skin causes digestive upset. Good fried and marinated.

## In practice

- Slippery skin + ring + under pine = Suillus; peel the cap.
- The grainy suillus ([S. granulatus](/en/griby/maslenok-zernistyj/)) has no ring.
