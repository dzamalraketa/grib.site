## Identification

Dark hoof-shaped woody conk on mulberry and other hardwoods; perennial.

## Research

Polysaccharide extracts studied in Korean/Japanese oncology-adjacent trials (adjuvant immune parameters); evidence is preliminary.

## In practice

- Used in traditional decoctions; supplement standardization is poor.
