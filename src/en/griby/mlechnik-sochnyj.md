## Description

Cap 6–15 cm, convex then funnel-shaped; surface velvety, deep orange to brick-brown. Gills cream, crowded. Latex white, **extremely copious**, dripping at the slightest damage; does not change colour in air. Flesh firm.

## The fishy smell — its calling card

Cut a weeping milkcap and within seconds you smell **herring**: the flesh releases trimethylamine, the same amine behind the "fishy" odour of seafood. The smell cooks off; in the dried mushroom it is faint and pleasant. In China, dried volemus is a recognised seasoning with a "meaty" aroma.

## Cooking

Edible: the latex is mild, not acrid — the mushroom can be fried without soaking. In Russian practice it was more often salted. The firm flesh holds its shape well.

## Lookalikes

Similar orange-brown milkcaps (*L. hygrophoroides*, *L. corrugis* overseas) are also edible; there are no dangerous lookalikes. Identify volemus by the combination: velvety cap + copious white latex + fishy-smelling cut.
