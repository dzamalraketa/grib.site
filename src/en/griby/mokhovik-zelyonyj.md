## Identification

Olive-brown suede-like cap that cracks with age; yellow tubes staining blue-green on touch; pale stem without a net.

## Edibility

Edible, mild flavor; best young. Flesh softens quickly with age.

## In practice

- Taste-check doubted pieces: bitterness → [bitter bolete](/en/griby/zhelchnyj-grib/), discard.
- Old flabby fruit bodies are not worth taking.
