## Identification

Brown to umber cap 5–15 cm covered with **yellowish** (not white) velar warts; cream gills; broad yellowish ring; volva reduced to 1–2 scaly rings on a bulbous base. Boreal-montane, mainly under spruce.

## Toxicity

Ibotenic acid + muscimol — the muscimol-group syndrome: intoxication, visual disturbance, delirium, alternating agitation and sleep; onset 0.5–3 h. Documented poisonings incl. children (PMID 760400). No amatoxins — kidney and liver failure phases do not occur.

## In practice

- A brown amanita is not an edible amanita. Distinguish from the blusher *A. rubescens* (flesh stains pink) and the panther cap *A. pantherina* (pure white warts, white ring, smooth bulb).
- Treat poisoning as for panther cap: emergency care, observation; outcome usually favourable but delirium is hazardous.
