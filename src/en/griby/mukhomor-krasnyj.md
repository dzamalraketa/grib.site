## Identification

Red cap with white flecks — remnants of the universal veil; white gills; ring and volval remnants on the stem. The picture-book mushroom.

## Toxins and the picture of poisoning

Contains **ibotenic acid and muscimol**: 30 min – 3 h after eating come intoxication-like delirium, hallucinations, incoordination, agitation then sleep. Fatalities are rare but described, especially combined with other substances. The mushroom's psychoactive use is a documented but risky practice — dose content varies wildly.

## In practice

- Decorative but not for the basket.
- Be aware: folk "agaric therapy" recipes circulate online — the evidence base does not support them.
