## Identification

Brownish amanita: volva at the base, ring, and flesh **turning pink-red when bruised** — the key mark.

## Edibility — conditional

Edible cooked (the blushing flesh is normal); raw it's toxic. Most guides advise amanita-eating only for the experienced — the family includes the death cap.

## In practice

- Red-bruising amanita flesh + striate ring = blusher.
- If amanita recognition isn't fluent — leave the whole genus.
