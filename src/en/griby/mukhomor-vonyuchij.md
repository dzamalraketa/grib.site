## Identification

White cap, white gills, ring and volva; the smell is faintly unpleasant in mature specimens. A member of the white-amanita ("destroying angel") group together with *A. verna* and *A. bisporigera*.

## Why it is dangerous

Contains **amatoxins** — the same mechanism as the [death cap](/en/griby/blednaya-poganka/): delayed onset 6–18 h, gastrointestinal phase, false recovery, liver failure. Deaths are documented.

## In practice

- All-white amanitas = do not pick at all.
- The champignon check: champignon gills turn brown, never have a volva.
