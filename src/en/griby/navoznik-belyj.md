## Identification

Tall white cylindrical cap covered in shaggy scales; gills white → black, dissolving into ink.

## Edibility

Edible young while gills are still white. Autodigestion ("inking") starts within hours — cook immediately, don't store.

## In practice

- White gills still → edible; pink/black already → discard.
- Cook the same day; refrigeration does not stop inking.
