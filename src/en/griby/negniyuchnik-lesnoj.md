## Identification

Small reddish-to-ochre cap 2–6 cm; white attached gills; thin **rubbery** stem — fruit bodies dry up rather than rot. Saprotroph on leaf litter in all woods; waves after rain from May to October.

## Edibility

Edible caps only (stems too tough); bland. "Filler" mushroom — for bulking a fry-up or soup when nothing better is fruiting; in a poor year it is what fills the basket.

## In practice

- Little russet "troop" agaric on litter + rubbery stem + white gills = toughshank.
- A phenology marker: when it flushes, the real season is days away.
- Take young caps; old ones go leathery and bitter.
