## Identification

Orange funnel-shaped caps in clusters on wood; sharp true gills running down the stem; flesh pale. A Mediterranean/southern species — known as a chanterelle lookalike for southern foragers.

## Why it is dangerous

Contains **illudin**: severe vomiting, abdominal cramps and diarrhea within hours. Recoveries are typical but the course is harsh — and in the dark the gills faintly glow, hence the name.

## How not to confuse it

| Feature | [Chanterelle](/en/griby/lisichka/) | Jack-o'-lantern |
|---|---|---|
| Underside | blunt ridges | sharp true gills |
| Growth | soil, mycorrhizal | wood, clusters |
| Flesh | dense, fruity smell | pale, no aroma |
