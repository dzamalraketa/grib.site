## Identification

Dense wood clusters; brown speckled caps; stems with a ring; white spore print.

## Edibility — conditional

Edible cooked — undercooked honey fungus causes gastrointestinal illness. Caps are best; stems get woody.

## The deadly double

- [Bordered galerina](/en/griby/galerina-okaymlennaya/): smaller, fragile ring, rusty spores — amatoxins.
- When in doubt: a white spore print is honey fungus; rusty is danger.
