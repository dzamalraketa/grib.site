## Identification

Small tough mushroom of grassland: tan cap with a central bump, widely-spaced cream gills, wiry stem. Grows in rings.

## Edibility

Edible — caps are used, stems discarded; pleasant sweetish flavor.

## How not to confuse it

- [Fool's funnel](/en/griby/govorushka-obelenaya/): decurrent crowded gills — muscarine poison.
- [Livid entoloma](/en/griby/entoloma-yadovitaya/): pink gills, bigger flesh.
