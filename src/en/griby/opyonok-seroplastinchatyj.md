## Identification

Clusters on conifer wood; cap pale yellow-brown; gills start pale and mature to smoky gray-lilac — the key field mark.

## Edibility

Edible caps. The species name capnoides refers to the smoke-gray gills.

## How not to confuse it

- [Sulphur tuft](/en/griby/lozhnoopyonok-sernozhyoltyj/): greenish-yellow gills, bitter — poisonous.
- Gray mature gills + conifer wood = this species.
