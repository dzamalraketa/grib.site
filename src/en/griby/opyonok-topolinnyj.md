## Identification

Clusters on poplar/willow wood: brown cap, ring on the stem, gills browning with age. Cultivated commercially as "pioppino."

## Edibility

Good edible — firm and nutty; caps and young stems used.

## In practice

- Poplar wood + brown cap + ring = pioppino.
- As always with clustered wood mushrooms, compare against the [bordered galerina](/en/griby/galerina-okaymlennaya/) — smaller, rusty spores, no real ring.
