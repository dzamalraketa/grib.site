## Identification

Silvery to lilac cap 3–8 cm, silky-fibrillose; young gills **violet**, rusting with spores; lilac stipe with cortina remnants. Mycorrhizal with spruce, pine and birch on acid soils; July–September, boreal.

## Edibility — "yes, but"

Genuinely edible — bland and uncommon. The card exists for the clarification:

- The genus is huge; some members are edible (*alboviolaceus*, *C. caperatus*), others are lethal (*C. orellanus*, *C. rubellus*).
- Edible and deadly share the rusty spores and cobweb veil — visual sorting is expert work.

## In practice

- "No webcaps for the pot" remains the rule; this card explains why the rule is a shortcut, not a taxonomy.
- Rarely collected — uncommon and flavour-poor; a specialist's mushroom.
