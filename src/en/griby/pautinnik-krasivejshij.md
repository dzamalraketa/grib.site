## Identification

Tawny to reddish-brown cap 3–8 cm with darker fibrillose scales; rusty gills; yellowish zigzag cortina bands on the stipe. Under pine and spruce on damp acid ground — a boreal species.

## Toxicity — deadly

Contains **orellanine**: delayed-onset acute kidney injury appearing days to weeks after the meal, progressing to chronic renal failure in a large share of cases. The toxin survives all cooking.

## In practice

- Small rusty-brown webcaps in conifer woods: never pick for food.
- The 2008 Nicholas Evans family poisoning — four people, AKI, transplant — is the canonical modern case.
- Kidney failure 1–2 weeks after eating wild mushrooms: tell the doctor about webcaps specifically.
