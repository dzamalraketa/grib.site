## Identification

A red fleshy bracket on oak, moist, weeping red juice; underside — separate tubes, not gills.

## Edibility

Edible — sourish, meaty texture; sliced thin and cooked. A "shock value" edible that is safe when recognized.

## In practice

- Red flesh + oak + red juice = beefsteak fungus; nothing poisonous mimics it well.
