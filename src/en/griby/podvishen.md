## Description

Cap 4–10 cm, white to greyish-cream, convex → depressed with a thin wavy edge, matt. Gills white → **pinkish** (pink spore print!). Stem short, central. Flesh white, dense. **Smell — fresh flour** (farinaceous) — the signature trait.

## "The miller" and the floury smell

The English name — "the miller" — is for the flour: the cut flesh smells of freshly ground grain. The smell is the most reliable field mark: no similar white mushroom smells so.

## Lookalikes — why this zone is dangerous

White mushroom + pink gills — where the deadly and the edible cross:
- **Livid entoloma**: same pink print, but larger, fleshier cap, no floury smell.
- **Destroying angel**: ring + volva — the miller has neither.
- **White clitocybes**: white print, not pink.
The rule: pink print + floury smell = miller; pink print without smell = stop.

## Cooking

Edible — a good autumn mushroom: fried, souped, pickled. But gather only with confidence — the "white gilled" zone costs too much to err in.
