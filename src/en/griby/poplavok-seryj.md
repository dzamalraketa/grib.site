## Identification

Grey cap 5–10 cm with a **striated margin**; free white gills; grey stipe **without a ring**; base enclosed in a broad sack-like volva. Mycorrhizal broadly; June–October, common.

## Edibility — conditional, expert-only

No amatoxins and genuinely eaten after cooking in Russian and Italian tradition. The catch is fatal context: young grisettes are "eggs" indistinguishable from *A. phalloides* eggs, and several vaginata-group relatives exist. "Edible" here assumes you can read a volva — most people can't.

## In practice

- Striated grey cap + no ring + saccate volva = grisette; even so, leave it.
- If ever eaten: adult open caps only, volva checked, well cooked.
- Best use of the species: training material for reading amanita volvas and rings before trusting any "edible amanita" claim.
