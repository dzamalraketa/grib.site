## Identification

Whitish-brown cap, pale clay gills turning rusty, a stem with a violet tinge and a faint ring zone — remnants of the cortina.

## Edibility

Edible and collected late in the season when little else remains.

## In practice

- *Cortinarius* has poisonous members (see the [fool's webcap](/en/griby/pautinnik-oranzhevo-krasnyj/)) — the violet stem and bog habitat are the safe markers.
