## Description

Cap 0.5–2.5 cm, conical to bell-shaped, with a sharp **nipple-like papilla** (hence *semilanceata* — "half-spear-shaped"); colour ochre-brown, nearly black when wet, drying to straw. The cap margin is initially enrolled, often showing faint gill-lines. Gills adnate, pale at first, then **lilac-black** (spore print dark purplish-brown). Stem 4–9 cm, very slender, yellowish, flexible; bruises faintly **blue** — a sign of psilocybin oxidation, though the reaction is weak and unreliable.

## Legal status and risk

In Russia and most countries psilocybin is a controlled substance; collecting or possessing this mushroom is prosecuted. We do not describe dosages or use. This card exists because **lookalike confusion kills**: people searching pastures for liberty caps regularly pick deadly species into the same basket.

## Lookalikes — from unpleasant to lethal

- **Pholiotina rugosa** (formerly *Conocybe filaris*): also small, brown, conical — but with a **ring** and a rusty spore print; contains **amatoxins**. A few of these in a handful of "liberty caps" means amatoxin poisoning (see `amatoxin-treatment-enjalbert-2002`).
- **Galerina marginata**: rusty print, grows on moss and wood — amatoxin-bearing.
- **Panaeolus species**: dark print but no papilla; inedible, causes GI upset.
- **Anonymous LBMs** (little brown mushrooms): dozens of lawn species are visually indistinguishable — separating them without microscopy is guesswork.

## Ecology

A saprotroph on decaying grass roots — hence its tie to old, unploughed pasture and upland grass. Fruits late: peak October–November after the first frosts and rains. In Russia it occurs in the north-west; the great strongholds are Britain, Ireland and the Pacific Northwest.

## Why "leave the liberty caps alone"

Beyond legal risk, this mushroom is a textbook case of identification-by-habit: a forager sees a familiar silhouette and skips checking print and ring. Our cards for *Pholiotina rugosa* and *Galerina marginata* show the point: the lookalikes do not merely resemble liberty caps — they **contain the same amatoxin as the death cap**.
