## Identification

Gray cap, white→pale-yellow gills, whitish stem with yellow tones; smell mealy. Under pines, late season.

## Edibility

Good edible — the yellow-tinged gills distinguish it from poisonous gray tricholomas.

## In practice

- Gray cap + yellowish gills + pine forest + late autumn = grey tricholoma.
- Without the yellow tinge — compare against [tiger tricholoma](/en/griby/ryadovka-tigrovaya/) (scaly, white-gilled, poisonous).
