## Identification

Gray cap covered with darker scales, white-gray gills, whitish stem. Confused with edible tricholomas — see [gray tricholoma](/en/griby/ryadovka-seraya/) and [wood blewit](/en/griby/ryadovka-fioletovaya/).

## Why it is dangerous

Gastrointestinal poisoning: per published data symptoms begin 15 minutes – 2 hours after eating — vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain; recovery takes 4–6 days. Several hospitalizations are described.

## In practice

- Gray-scaled "blewit-like" mushrooms with white gills — leave; edible blewits have lilac tones.
