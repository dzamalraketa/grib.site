## Identification

Orange zoned cap that greens with age and handling; carrot-orange latex turning red then green in 10–30 min; flesh staining green on the cut. Mycorrhizal **only with spruce**.

## Edibility

Edible — fried, salted, pickled; slightly more bitter than *L. deliciosus*, especially old greening specimens. Pick firm young caps.

## In practice

- Spruce nearby + green-staining latex and cap = *deterrimus*.
- The pine saffron milkcap *L. deliciosus* is milder and greener-stained less; confusion between saffron milkcaps is harmless — all are edible.
- Inedible orange milkcaps (e.g. *L. helvus*, *L. torminosus*) have white or acrid latex, not orange.
