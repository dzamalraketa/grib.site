## Identification

Carrot-orange funnel cap with concentric zones; cut flesh exudes orange latex turning green; brittle texture; under pines.

## Edibility

A classic edible: fried, salted, pickled. The green staining on handled fruit bodies is normal.

## In practice

- Orange + latex + under pine → saffron milkcap; the spruce form is similar and also good.
