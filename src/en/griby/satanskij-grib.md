## Identification

Pale grayish-olive cap, red pore surface, thick stem with red mesh; flesh whitish, **turns blue when cut**; mature fruit bodies smell unpleasant. Lookalikes among pale boletes are separated by the red pores and the smell.

## Why it is dangerous

Contains **bolesatine** — a toxin causing strong gastrointestinal poisoning: vomiting, diarrhea, pain. Sources differ on severity after thorough cooking; the claim "harmless when boiled" is not proven, so treat it as poisonous.

## In practice

- Pale bolete + red pores + blue-staining flesh = discard.
- Do not confuse with the [bilious bolete](/en/griby/zhelchnyj-grib/): that one has pink tubes and bitter flesh.
