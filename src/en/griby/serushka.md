## Identification

Fleshy grey-pink to lilac-grey cap 5–12 cm with darker concentric zones; white acrid latex that greys on the gills. With birch in damp mixed woods — September to November, frost-tolerant.

## Edibility — conditional

A salted mushroom after 2–3 days of soaking or boiling in Russian and Finnish tradition; Western guides treat it cautiously. The practical issue is maggots rather than toxicity — late-fruiting bodies are often infested before fully opening.

## In practice

- Grey-pink zoned cap + white acrid latex + late autumn + birch = grey milkcap.
- Pick only young firm specimens; it may be the only mass species left in an empty November wood.
