## Identification

White cap, gills **pink → chocolate-brown**, thin ring, white flesh. On grassland soil.

## Edibility

Edible and fine — the cultivated bisporus' wild cousin.

## How not to confuse it — two checks

- Yellow staining at the stem base + chemical smell → [yellow-stainer](/en/griby/shampinon-zheltokozhij/), discard.
- Any volva/sac at the base → could be an amanita button — excavate the base, don't pull.
