## Identification

White champignon-like mushroom; the cap and especially the stem base **turn bright yellow when cut or pressed**; smell phenolic, "chemical". Gills pink → brown.

## Why it is dangerous

Gastrointestinal syndrome: vomiting, diarrhea, cramps within 1–2 hours. Sensitivity varies — some people tolerate it, but the species consistently accounts for urban mushroom poisonings.

## How not to confuse it

| Feature | [Field mushroom](/en/griby/shampinon-polevoj/) | Yellow-stainer |
|---|---|---|
| Cut flesh | whitish, maybe slightly pink | instantly bright yellow |
| Smell | mushroomy, anise | phenolic, inky |
| Habitat | meadows, forest edges | lawns, gardens, city soil |
