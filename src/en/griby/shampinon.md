## Identification

The familiar white/brown cultivated mushroom; cremini and portobello are the same species at different ages.

## Edibility

Edible cooked — the industry standard. UV-exposed mushrooms carry meaningful vitamin D2 (the industry uses this).

## In practice

- The wild counterpart for foragers is the [field mushroom](/en/griby/shampinon-polevoj/).
- Bought mushrooms: cook — raw agaricus carries agaritine, which cooking reduces.
