## Identification

Large white funnel, woolly cap margin, crowded decurrent gills, sparse acrid latex.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — salting after soaking, like its milkcap kin.

## In practice

- Woolly edge + decurrent crowded gills + birch = fleecy milkcap.
