## Identification

A wrinkled brown cap hanging **free** on a tall stem — the skirt attaches only at the very top, unlike fused morels.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — thorough cooking required; raw or undercooked it causes gastrointestinal and neurological symptoms. A subset of people react even cooked.

## In practice

- Free-hanging thimble cap = Verpa; fused ridges = [morel](/en/griby/smorchok/).
- First meal — a small portion, cooked well.
