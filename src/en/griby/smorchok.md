## Identification

A honeycomb cap of pits and ridges **fused** to the stem; completely hollow when sliced — the single most reliable field mark.

## Edibility

Edible cooked — a delicacy. Raw or undercooked morels poison: a 2023 Montana restaurant outbreak (51 ill, 2 dead) implicated undercooked morels, documented by CDC MMWR.

## In practice

- Hollow + fused ridges = true morel.
- Always cook — even fine-dining raw morel prep is a documented hazard.
