## Identification

A large cream mass of flattened ruffled lobes at a pine base — looks exactly like a cauliflower or an egg noodle clump.

## Edibility

A choice edible when young and cream-colored; the folds trap debris, so it must be washed carefully before cooking.

## In practice

- At a pine base + noodle-fold mass = unmistakable.
- Older yellowing specimens toughen; take firm pale ones.
