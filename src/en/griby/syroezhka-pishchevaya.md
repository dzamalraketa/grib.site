## Identification

Pinkish-tan russula; the cap skin peels only partway — a famous detail ("the flirt"). Flesh white, mild, nutty.

## Edibility

Edible — mild taste confirms it; hot/acrid taste in a russula is a warning (see [sickener](/en/griby/syroezhka-rvotnaya/)).

## In practice

- Taste a crumb raw: mild = edible group, sharp/hot = discard.
- Skin peeling halfway = R. vesca specifically.
