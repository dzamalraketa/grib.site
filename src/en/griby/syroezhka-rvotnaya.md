## Identification

Bright red cap that peels easily, white gills, brittle flesh. The taste is sharply acrid — a nibble-and-spit test (experienced identifiers only) burns the tongue.

## Why it is dangerous

The acrid compounds irritate the gut: vomiting, nausea, abdominal pain within a couple of hours. Recovery is usually quick, but the species is classed as poisonous in current guides.

## In practice

- Red russula on wet conifer ground — skip; edible russulas look different.
- See the [green russula](/en/griby/syroezhka-zelyonaya/) and [shrimp russula](/en/griby/syroezhka-pishchevaya/) for comparison of edible relatives.
