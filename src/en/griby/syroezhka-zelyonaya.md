## Identification

Green cap with a cracked/areolate surface; white brittle gills; no ring, no volva.

## Edibility

Edible — mild. But the green amanita problem is real.

## The critical check

- [Death cap](/en/griby/blednaya-poganka/) can be green-capped: look for **volva at the base + ring + white gills on a stemmed amanita structure** — excavate the base of every green cap before deciding.
- Green russula breaks chalk-brittle; amanita flesh cuts.
