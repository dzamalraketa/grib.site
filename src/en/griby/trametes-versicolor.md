## Identification

Thin leathery shelves with concentric color zones; white pore underside.

## The clinical dossier

PSK (krestin) is an approved adjuvant cancer therapy in Japan, used alongside chemotherapy since the 1980s; PSP is the Chinese counterpart. This is the strongest human evidence base of any medicinal mushroom.

## In practice

- True turkey tail: thin, flexible, white pores. False lookalikes (Stereum) are smooth underneath and not pored.
- Wild-harvested material varies; clinical products are standardized extracts.
