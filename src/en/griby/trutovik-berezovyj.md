## Identification

Pale smooth brackets on birch only; white pore underside; firm corky flesh.

## History

Found among Ötzi's belongings (5300 BP) — possibly used as tinder or medicine (he carried intestinal parasites the fungus affects in vitro).

## Compounds

Piptamine, betulinic acid precursors, polysaccharides — preclinical activity documented.

## In practice

- Pale bracket + birch only = birch polypore.
- Historically used as a styptic bandage too.
