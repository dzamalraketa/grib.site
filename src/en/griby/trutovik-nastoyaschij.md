## Identification

Grey hoof-shaped perennial conk on birch/beech; layered interior — the source of amadou tinder and felt.

## History

Used for millennia: fire-starting tinder, felt hats, wound staunching; Ötzi carried a piece alongside birch polypore.

## Evidence

Traditional decoctions exist; preclinical studies on polysaccharides are early-stage.

## In practice

- Grey hoof on birch/beech, woody layers inside = tinder fungus.
