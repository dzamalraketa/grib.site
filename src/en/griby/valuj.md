## Identification

Yellow-brown slimy cap, funnel at maturity; unpleasant smell (the species name says it).

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — salted after soaking; the smell largely vanishes in brine.

## In practice

- Slimy yellow-brown + foul smell + birch woods = valuj.
