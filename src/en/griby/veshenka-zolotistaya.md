## Identification

Bright yellow clustered caps with decurrent gills — a vivid oyster relative. Wild in East Asia on elm and other hardwoods; cultivated worldwide.

## Edibility

Edible; delicate, cooks quickly; aroma fades fast — cook fresh.

## Research note

Unusually high in ergothioneine, an antioxidant amino acid; a 2025 Japanese human study examined effects on gut microbiome composition. Interesting, early-stage data.
