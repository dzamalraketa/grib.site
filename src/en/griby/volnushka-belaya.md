## Identification

White to cream-ochre cap 4–10 cm with a woolly/fringed margin but no pink zoning; white acrid latex unchanging on air. With birch in damp places — August to October, abundant in wet years.

## Edibility — conditional

Same protocol as the woolly milkcap: 2–3 days of soaking with water changes, then hot-brined after blanching. Rated below *L. torminosus* — looser flesh, more persistent bitterness.

## In practice

- Whitish cap + woolly edge + birch + damp ground = bearded milkcap.
- From *L. vellereus* (fleecy milkcap): the pubescens margin is woolly, and latex is present — vellereus is drier and thicker.
- Goes into mixed salted-mushroom tubs alongside the pink species; rarely salted alone.
