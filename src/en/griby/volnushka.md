## Identification

Pinkish cap with a strongly woolly/fringed margin; white acrid latex; under birch.

## Edibility — conditional

Conditionally edible — the salting tribe; soaked then salted.

## In practice

- Pink + woolly fringe + birch = woolly milkcap.
