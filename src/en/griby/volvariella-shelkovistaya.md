## Identification

White silky cap on wood; **volva at the base**; gills free, turning pink — the volvariella signature.

## Edibility

Edible — but the volva demands respect: amanitas carry the same organ.

## In practice

- Volva + pink gills + on wood = volvariella.
- Volva + white gills + on soil = suspect an amanita — see the [death cap](/en/griby/blednaya-poganka/).
