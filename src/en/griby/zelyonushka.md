## Identification

Yellow cap with a greenish tinge, sulfur-yellow gills, whitish-yellow stem. Grows under pines on sandy soils, late in the season.

## What the clinic showed

Since 1992 France has recorded 12 cases of delayed rhabdomyolysis after meals rich in *Tricholoma equestre*. In Bedry et al. (N Engl J Med 2001) the link was considered clear: 1–3 days after eating came muscle weakness, pain and muscle-tissue breakdown threatening kidneys and heart. Some cases were severe; there were deaths. A later series (PMC5287993) confirms the picture.

## In practice

- Not "the best fried mushroom" — repeated large portions are the documented risk pattern.
- Muscle pain, weakness and dark urine after a mushroom meal — hospital, with a mention of this species.
