## Identification

White cap 7–15 cm covered in coarse rusty-brown scales; tall stipe with bulbous base and movable ring; flesh **stains red/orange on cutting**. Saprotroph on rich soil — parks, gardens, edges; July–October.

## Edibility — conditional

Cooked and eaten by most people without issue — but a real minority gets vomiting and diarrhoea 1–4 h later even after thorough cooking. Unpredictable: first taste should be a small solo portion. The North American/subtropical relative *C. molybdites* (green spore print) is properly toxic — always check the print is white.

## In practice

- Differs from *Macrolepiota procera*: stockier, coarser scales, **red-staining** flesh, no snakeskin pattern on the stipe.
- Take caps only — the stipe is fibrous.
