Safety Assessment of HEA-Enriched Cordyceps cicadae Mycelium:
A Randomized Clinical Trial. Journal of the American College
of Nutrition. 2021;40(2):127–132.

## Rationale

*Cordyceps cicadae* is an entomopathogenic fungus parasitizing cicada nymphs; tradition attributes antitumor, antifatigue and nephro-/hepatoprotective action to it. Industrial liquid-fermentation mycelium production gives stable raw material with a marker content of N6-(2-hydroxyethyl)adenosine (HEA) — but preclinical toxicology alone does not answer whether the product is safe in humans. Clinical safety reports for fermented mycelium were scarce at the time.

## Methods

A randomized clinical safety study. 49 participants took granules of lyophilized HEA-enriched *C. cicadae* mycelium — 1.05 g once daily for 3 months. Blood was drawn before and after the course. Panel: kidney and liver function markers, blood electrolytes, lipid profile. Subjective complaints and adverse events were recorded under clinical observation.

## Results

- **Biochemistry:** no significant differences between baseline and final kidney and liver function measurements.
- **Electrolytes and lipids:** no effect on blood electrolytes or lipid profile recorded.
- **Tolerance:** under clinical observation, participants reported no adverse effects or complaints.

## Conclusions

The authors conclude that HEA-enriched liquid-fermented *C. cicadae* mycelium at 1.05 g/day for 3 months is safe and can be considered raw material for functional products.

## Worth noting

**Strengths:** a study devoted entirely to safety — a rare design for mushroom raw material; objective laboratory endpoints (kidney, liver, electrolytes, lipids) instead of subjective questionnaires.

**Limitations:**
1. **3 months at 1.05 g/day** — one dose and a short horizon; chronic intake safety (a year or more) and dose dependence were not studied.
2. **One intake arm** in the abstract; how randomization and control were arranged is not disclosed in the abstract — methodological detail requires the full text.
3. **Healthy sample:** n=49 without serious disease — does not answer safety in liver/kidney patients, pregnant women or children.
4. **Product specificity:** HEA-enriched fermented mycelium ≠ dried wild cordyceps and ≠ extracts from other manufacturers with different adenosine-compound profiles.
