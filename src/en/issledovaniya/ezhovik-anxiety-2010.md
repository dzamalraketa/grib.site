Nagano M., Shimizu K., Kondo R., Hayashi C., Sato D., Kitagawa K., Ohnuki K. Reduction of depression and anxiety by 4 weeks Hericium erinaceus intake. Biomed Res. 2010;31(4):231–237.

## Rationale

The fruiting body of Hericium erinaceus contains hericenones and erinacines — low-molecular-weight compounds that stimulate nerve growth factor (NGF) synthesis — giving grounds to expect effects on central nervous system function and autonomic regulation. This work assessed the clinical effects of 4 weeks of H. erinaceus on menopausal symptoms, depression, sleep quality and nonspecific complaints — states in whose pathogenesis autonomic and neurotrophic imbalance plays a meaningful role.

## Methods

A randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial: 30 women, predominantly in the menopausal period, received cookies with H. erinaceus fruiting-body powder (4 cookies × 0.5 g = 2.0 g/day) or identical placebo cookies for 4 weeks. Scales: Kupperman Menopausal Index (KMI), CES-D depression scale, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) and Indefinite Complaints Index (ICI).

## Results

After the course, the active group's CES-D and total ICI scores fell significantly from baseline; on the ICI subscales "insentive" and "palpitatio" the active group significantly outperformed placebo, while "concentration," "irritating" and "anxious" showed only trends. No adverse events were recorded.

## Conclusions

Four weeks of H. erinaceus intake reduced depression scores and nonspecific complaints in this group — a modest but real signal in a population prone to mood and autonomic symptoms.

## Worth noting

The honest framing matters: significant effects appeared on the total ICI and two subscales, not on the headline menopausal or sleep measures — KMI and PSQI did not clearly move. The delivery form (cookies with 2 g of powder) is culinary rather than pharmacological — a strength for real-world relevance and a weakness for standardization. n=30 in a symptom-scale study detects only fairly large effects; the trend-level subscales should not be read as failed proof or proof.
