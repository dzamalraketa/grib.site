Vigna L., Morelli F., Agnelli G.M., Napolitano F., Ratto D., Occhinegro A., Di Iorio C., Savino E., Girometta C., Brandalise F., Rossi P. *Hericium erinaceus* Improves Mood and Sleep Disorders in Patients Affected by Overweight or Obesity: Could Circulating Pro-BDNF and BDNF Be Potential Biomarkers? Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine. 2019;2019:7861297.

## Rationale

Depression, anxiety and sleep disturbance frequently co-occur with overweight and obesity; the authors tested whether H. erinaceus mycelium affects these symptoms and measured circulating pro-BDNF and BDNF — a rare mechanistic datapoint in a human mushroom trial.

## Methods

An open-label pilot: 77 participants with BMI ≥ 25 took H. erinaceus mycelium 3 g/day for 8 weeks. Depression, anxiety and sleep-quality scales plus circulating pro-BDNF/BDNF were measured before and after.

## Results

Depression, anxiety and sleep scores improved during intake; circulating pro-BDNF and BDNF levels rose.

## Conclusions

Symptomatic improvement alongside a neurotrophin-family signal — a plausible-mechanism datapoint for the species in a metabolically compromised group.

## Worth noting

Open-label, no placebo: the mood improvements cannot be separated from expectation and regression to the mean. The pro-BDNF/BDNF rise is the more interesting output — human biomarker data of this kind for lion's mane are rare. A controlled trial in this population is the obvious next step.
