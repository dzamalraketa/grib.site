Front Nutr 2025. Benefits, side effects, and uses of Hericium erinaceus as a supplement: a systematic review (PMID 40959699).

## Rationale

Lion's mane is sold as a "brain mushroom", yet the human literature is scattered: isolated small RCTs, pilots, lab work. This is the first PRISMA review pooling **all clinical** H. erinaceus data with bias-risk assessment (ROBIS) — a real attempt to answer "what is proven vs what is marketing".

## Methods

- PubMed search 2000–06/2024 per PRISMA; PROSPERO registration (CRD42024571250).
- Quality control with the **ROBIS** tool.
- Included: **5 RCTs, 3 pilot clinical trials, 15 laboratory studies, 1 cohort study, 1 case report, 1 computational analysis**.
- Topics: cognition, mood, SCFA-producing microbiota, neural stimulation, safety.

## What the review gathered

- **Cognition**: modest positive signals in older adults with MCI and in healthy adults — but short courses (4–16 weeks), small n, heterogeneous extract forms (fruiting body vs erinacine-enriched mycelium).
- **Mood**: isolated positive studies in mild anxiety/depression — again, small samples.
- **Microbiota**: data on SCFA-producing bacteria growth — mostly laboratory and pilot work.
- **Safety**: side effects rare and mild (GI discomfort); no serious signals in the clinical data.

## Caveats

- The review confirms the overall picture of our lion's mane card series: **interesting signals exist, "miracle" does not**. That is not a refutation of benefit — it is "the evidence is thin so far".
- Heterogeneity of forms (fruiting bodies, mycelium, erinacine-enriched mycelium) is the main methodological noise: effects of different preparations cannot be lumped together.
- Null results (our card `ezhovik-working-memory-young-adults-2022`, PMID 40276537) fit the review picture: acute and short interventions give weak or zero response.
- The practical takeaway: at the current evidence level lion's mane is a **reasonably safe supplement with unconfirmed but investigable benefit** — not a treatment for cognitive impairment or depression.
