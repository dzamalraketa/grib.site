J Nutr 2012. Ergocalciferol from mushrooms or supplements consumed with a standard meal increases 25-hydroxyergocalciferol but decreases 25-hydroxycholecalciferol in the serum of healthy adults (PMID 22623385).

## Rationale

Dietary vitamin D is scarce, and UV-irradiated mushrooms accumulate ergocalciferol (D2). The practical question: does "mushroom" D2 work as well as the pharmacy kind when eaten with an ordinary meal?

## Methods

38 volunteers randomized to 4 arms for 6 weeks, intake with a standard meal:

- **C** (control): untreated mushrooms, 0.85 µg/day D2, n=10;
- **M1**: UV mushrooms, 8.8 µg/day, n=10;
- **M2**: UV mushrooms, 17.1 µg/day, n=9;
- **S**: purified ergocalciferol + untreated mushrooms, 28.2 µg/day total, n=9.

Serum 25(OH)D, 25(OH)D2 and 25(OH)D3 were measured.

## Results

- **D2 is absorbed from mushrooms**: 25(OH)D2 rose by +13.8 (M1), +12.7 (M2) and +32.8 (S) nmol/L vs +1.2 in control.
- **But 25(OH)D3 fell**: −10.4 (M1), −20.6 (M2), −29.5 (S) nmol/L — the more D2, the stronger the D3 displacement.
- Net result: **total vitamin D status unchanged** — the D2 gain was offset by the D3 loss.

## Conclusions

The authors state that ergocalciferol from UV-treated mushrooms is absorbed and metabolized, but in adults with an adequate baseline status (83 nmol/L) it does not raise total 25(OH)D — D2 "displaces" D3 from the bloodstream.

## Caveats

- This is one of the key "honest" papers in the vitamin-D-mushroom series: mushroom D2 **is genuinely bioavailable**, but the promise "eat mushrooms, get more vitamin D" did not hold in well-nourished adults.
- D2↔D3 competition explains the divergence between studies: isolated "D2 intake" looks effective while total status does not change.
- The M1–M2 doses (~9–17 µg/day) are close to real portions of store-bought UV mushrooms — applied, not exaggerated lab data.
- Contrast with the 2016 meta-analysis (our card `mushroom-uv-vitamind-meta-2016`): the effect appears where baseline deficiency exists (European cohorts ~39 nmol/L) and vanishes where status is already adequate.
