Wachtel-Galor S., Tomlinson B., Benzie I.F.F. Ganoderma lucidum
('Lingzhi'), a Chinese medicinal mushroom: biomarker responses
in a controlled human supplementation study. Br J Nutr.
2004;91(2):263–269.

## Rationale

The group's acute study showed antioxidant absorption; the obvious next question — does daily intake change anything durable? This 4-week crossover asked it directly.

## Methods

A double-blind placebo-controlled crossover: 18 healthy adults took 1.44 g/day of a commercial encapsulated reishi preparation (≈13.2 g fresh-mushroom equivalent) or placebo for 4 weeks per phase, measuring antioxidant status, lipids, DNA damage, immune and inflammatory markers.

## Results

No biomarker — antioxidant status, lipids, DNA damage, immunity, inflammation — changed significantly versus placebo. Tolerability was clean throughout.

## Conclusions

Four weeks of reishi at this dose changed nothing measurable — an honest null that directly contradicts the acute absorption signal's implied promise.

## Worth noting

This is the kind of negative replication the supplement literature needs and rarely publishes. The contrast is instructive: acute antioxidant capacity demonstrably rises after a dose; nothing persists or accumulates over a month. The later 6-month enriched-preparation RCT found the positive this study missed — dose enrichment and duration are the candidate explanations.
