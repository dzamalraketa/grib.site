## Species, names and observations

- [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/) brings together occurrence records from collections, research projects and observations.
- [MycoBank](https://www.mycobank.org/) records scientific fungal names and their publication details.
- [First Nature](https://www.first-nature.com/fungi/) is an illustrated field guide with an emphasis on Britain and Europe.
- [MushroomExpert](https://www.mushroomexpert.com/) is Michael Kuo’s guide to visible and microscopic features, with an emphasis on North America.
- [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/) collects observations and community identifications. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Main_Page) is a resource for finding images and their credits and licences.

## Studies, reviews and registries

Our [research catalog]({{ '/en/issledovaniya/' | url }}) contains {{ collections.issledovaniyaEn.length }} entries with study designs, findings and links to publications or preprints.

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) indexes biomedical literature. [PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) archives full texts.
- [Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/products-and-services/cochrane-library) publishes systematic reviews and explains how to access them.
- [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) and [DRKS](https://drks.de/search/en) document registered studies and planned outcomes. Look for a published paper or results posted in the registry to see what the study found.
- The [US National Cancer Institute](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/cam/hp/mushrooms-pdq) summarizes research on mushrooms in oncology. [MSK About Herbs](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/diagnosis-treatment/symptom-management/integrative-medicine/herbs/search) provides clinical reference entries on supplements.

## Authors, books and reading lists

- [Paul Stamets’s publication list](https://paulstamets.com/peer-reviewed-scientific-articles) and [Fungi Perfecti’s research page](https://fungi.com/pages/research-and-development) help locate papers. We check findings against the journal publication.
- [MushroomReferences](https://mushroomreferences.com/) is a curated reading list. We cite the original study when discussing its findings. Our [guide to Paul Stamets]({{ '/en/temy/stamets/' | url }}) covers his books and ideas.

## How to read our references

A field guide, an observation and a clinical study answer different questions. We check species names and descriptions against taxonomic databases and guides. For claims about nutrition, ecology or applications, we identify the study and explain what it measured. We label preprints, books and company materials by source type.
