<section>
  <h2>Academic databases</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>PubMed</strong> — the largest biomedical publication database (over 36 million records). <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</a></li>
    <li><strong>PubMed Central (PMC)</strong> — open-access full-text archive of biomedical journals. <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc</a></li>
    <li><strong>Cochrane Library</strong> — systematic reviews prepared to Cochrane methodology. <a href="https://www.cochranelibrary.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">cochranelibrary.com</a></li>
    <li><strong>National Cancer Institute (PDQ)</strong> — official NCI information on complementary approaches in oncology. <a href="https://www.cancer.gov/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">cancer.gov</a></li>
    <li><strong>Frontiers</strong> — a family of open-access peer-reviewed journals. <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">frontiersin.org</a></li>
    <li><strong>MDPI</strong> — publisher of open-access peer-reviewed journals (quality varies by journal). <a href="https://www.mdpi.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">mdpi.com</a></li>
    <li><strong>ResearchGate</strong> — a network where authors publish preprints and discussion. <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">researchgate.net</a></li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Authors and organizations</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Paul Stamets</strong> — American mycologist, author of “Mycelium Running” and “Fantastic Fungi”, co-founder of Fungi Perfecti. We use only the peer-reviewed section of publications from <a href="https://paulstamets.com" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">paulstamets.com</a>; sections unrelated to scientific work are not used.</li>
    <li><strong>Fungi Perfecti</strong> — Stamets’ mycelium and supplement company (<a href="https://fungi.com" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">fungi.com</a>). Used only as a source of patent data and publications.</li>
    <li><strong>Robert Beelman and the Penn State Center for Plant and Mushroom Foods for Health</strong> — authors of the hypothesis of ergothioneine as a “longevity vitamin”.</li>
    <li><strong>Wikimedia Commons</strong> — the source of openly licensed mushroom illustrations (<a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">commons.wikimedia.org</a>). Each species page lists the author, license and a link to the original.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Selection principles</h2>
  <p>When preparing material we follow simple rules:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>we use only peer-reviewed sources or preprints with an explicit status label;</li>
    <li>in research entries we separate the authors’ hypotheses from confirmed facts;</li>
    <li>we do not publish close paraphrases — only independent retellings;</li>
    <li>every entry links directly to the original so the reader can verify it.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
