  <h1 class="page-header__title">About the project</h1>
  <p class="page-header__lead">
    Mushroom World is an independent reference about fungi: a species
    catalog, popular-science reviews of mycology, ecology and practical
    uses. We collect and adapt open material, rely on peer-reviewed
    publications, and present it in a form suited for reading, citing
    and discussion.
  </p>
</header>

<section>
  <h2>What this site is</h2>
  <p>
    Mushroom World is an encyclopedic portal about fungi in the broad
    sense: cap and wood-decay species, yeasts and moulds, mycelium as an
    organism in its own right. Our focus is biology, ecology and applied
    fields — mycoremediation, mycorrhization in forestry, biological pest
    control with entomopathogenic fungi, and the use of beta-glucans and
    other bioactive compounds.
  </p>
  <p>
    The species catalog and the seasonal calendar are available in
    English. Articles and research-card reviews are currently published
    in <a href="/">the Russian version</a>.
  </p>

  <h2>How to cite</h2>
  <p>
    Pages may be quoted with attribution and a link to the source page.
    Each entry lists its primary sources at the bottom.
  </p>
</section>
